A Small Dictionaryof Ninety-Four Words

Editorial Note

Definitions are arranged according to common contemporary usage. Where a word has more than one established sense, senses are numbered separately. Examples have been written to illustrate ordinary use. Pronunciations are given in broad transcription.

How to read an entry

  • head the word being defined
  • pron. broad pronunciation
  • pos part of speech
  • 1, 2… numbered senses
  • ital. example of use
  • see relation to another entry
