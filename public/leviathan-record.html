From 13:00 JST on 1 October 2026, the Citizens opened one at a time, every 2 hours 40 minutes. Anyone could call execute() on an open Citizen, and 0.001 ETH was sent to the caller. The hundredth Citizen was executed at 13:00:11 JST on 12 October.

Eight were executed before the morning of 5 October, between 13 minutes and 34 hours after they opened. At 14:06 on 5 October, one transaction executed the 29 Citizens from #3 to #36 that were still open. Of the 63 Citizens from #37 on, 57 were executed in the block 11 seconds after they opened, 5 after 23 seconds and 1 after 35 seconds.

Eighteen minutes after the first Citizen opened, an address that executed nothing sent five transactions to three Citizens. On #19 and #79 it called upgradeTo(address) with contracts it had deployed seconds earlier, and both calls failed. On #25 and #79 it called initialize(), which succeeded and changed nothing. A fifth call to #79, with the selector 0x0000d00d, also failed. The address is 0x9c08257c0c535abdf83d1493cb6aa9db7a2cce9d.
